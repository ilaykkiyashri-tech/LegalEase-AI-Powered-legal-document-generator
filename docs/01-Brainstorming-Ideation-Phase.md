# Phase 1: Brainstorming & Ideation

## Problem Statement
Drafting legal documents (employment contracts, NDAs, lease agreements) typically requires legal expertise or paid professional services, creating a barrier for startup founders, freelancers, landlords, and individuals who need reliable documents quickly and affordably.

## Idea
**LegalEase** — an AI-powered legal document generator that uses Generative AI (Google Gemini) to produce customizable, accurate, and editable legal document templates from simple user inputs, without requiring any legal background.

## Target Users
- Startup founders hiring new employees
- Freelancers needing NDAs with clients
- Landlords drafting residential lease agreements
- Individuals and small businesses needing general legal documents

## Core Value Proposition
- No legal background needed
- Documents generated from a few structured inputs (document type, parties, terms, effective date)
- Editable preview before finalizing
- Professional formatting with custom branding (logo, fonts)
- Multi-format export: .TXT, .DOCX, .PDF

## Brainstormed User Stories / Scenarios
1. **Startup founder** enters new-hire details, selects "Employment Contract," and receives a comprehensive agreement (roles, responsibilities, compensation, confidentiality). Customizes wording, adds company logo, downloads as branded PDF.
2. **Freelancer** needs an NDA for a client. Specifies parties, confidentiality scope, and effective date; receives a structured, legally sound NDA instantly.
3. **Landlord** drafts a residential lease agreement by entering property address, tenant details, and lease terms; receives a formatted, editable document downloadable as .DOCX.

## Why Generative AI (Gemini)?
- Long context window supports structured, multi-part prompting
- Capable of generating formal, section-based legal text with consistent clause structure
- Fast generation speed suitable for an interactive tool

## Key Features Shortlisted for MVP
- Document type selection (Agreement, Contract, NDA, Lease Agreement, Employment Offer Letter)
- Structured inputs: parties involved, terms & conditions, effective date
- AI-generated draft with editable preview
- Branded export in TXT, DOCX, and PDF
